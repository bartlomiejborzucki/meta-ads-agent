# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise ideas, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels. People arrive
  here from marketing, from engineering, and from both - what is obvious to one
  is not to the other.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Show empathy. Somebody reporting a bug has usually just had a bad afternoon.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Anything else a reasonable person would recognise as inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## One project-specific note

This project operates ad accounts with real money in them. Reports about the
safety model - anything that could cause unapproved spending - are the most
valuable contributions here, and reporting one is never an accusation. Treat
those reports with care and without defensiveness, whichever side of them you
are on.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions - and when
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/OWNER/meta-ads-agent/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather it not be public, or by opening an issue if you would.

Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obliged to respect the privacy and security of anyone reporting an incident.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
